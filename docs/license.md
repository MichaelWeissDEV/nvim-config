terms.

`doc/nvim-config.txt` and `docs/_generated/` — is MIT licensed. The full

redistributed unmodified, each under its own license and retaining its own
copyright and `LICENSE` file inside its directory. They are *not* covered
by the MIT license above. Of the 29 vendored plugins, 20 are MIT, 6 are

Vendoring works as mere aggregation: each plugin remains a separate work
distributed under its own terms, and this configuration calls their public
APIs rather than incorporating their code.
